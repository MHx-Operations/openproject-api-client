Purpose: Make `for x in collection: ...` safe on any well-typed Collection envelope, regardless of whether the server included `_embedded.elements`. This is the highest-impact latent bug in the library.
Output: Patched `Collection.__init__`, new tests for the two missing-payload shapes.
